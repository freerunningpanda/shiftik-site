FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Shiftik free?

Yes. The calendar, alarms, pay, totals, widgets and backup are free and will stay free. Extra features come with PRO, a one-time purchase.

Do I need the internet?

No. Your schedule, alarms and pay work offline. The RuStore version goes online only to buy or restore PRO.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone: there's no sign-up, and your schedule and pay aren't sent anywhere. Details are in the privacy policy.

Will the alarm really ring?

Shiftik sets exact Android alarms and rings even when the app is closed. Some phones close apps to save battery — Alarm check shows what to set up and lets you set a test alarm.

How do I move to a new phone?

Save a backup to a file and restore it on the new phone: Settings → Backup. PRO comes back from the backup or with your purchase code.
